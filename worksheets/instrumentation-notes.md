# Instrumentation notes

Fill this in after step 5, in your own words. Two or three sentences each. Your
coach will ask about it at the show-and-tell. Keep it with your worksheet, as
`agents/<your folder>/readout/instrumentation-notes.md`.

1. Where does a trace start and end in your agent, and why there?

2. Which parts of a run became which kind of span, and why?

3. What metadata did you attach, and which customer question does it answer?

4. What would you see in the console if you had instrumented only the model call?
