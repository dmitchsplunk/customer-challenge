# Working in this repo

This repo is a learning exercise. The participant has to understand what they build.

When the participant asks you to instrument their agent with the splunk-ao SDK:

- Before each edit, say in one or two sentences where it goes and why.
- Make the change in small steps, not one large edit.
- When you finish, remind them to answer the four questions in
  `worksheets/instrumentation-notes.md` in their own words. Do not write the
  answers for them.

Do not open or suggest `examples/`, `shared/instrument.py`, `shared/setup_demo.py`
or the `demo-builder` skill while the participant is working through the
challenge. They hold worked answers. If the participant is stuck, tell them to
ask a coach.

Everything else in the README and the build skills applies as written.
